# PETi release-readiness control surface

The release plans require evidence rather than a feature flag alone. The
workspace now exposes the operational control points needed for that process:

- backend feature flags and emergency variable-cost switch;
- support codes and owner-scoped support cases;
- privacy export and account deletion APIs;
- backend Premium reconciliation boundary;
- source-traceable reports and assistant responses;
- species profile support independent from AI capability support.

Production rollout remains fail-closed until provider verification, billing,
privacy residual-data, security, accessibility, and Google Play evidence are
completed in the target environment.
