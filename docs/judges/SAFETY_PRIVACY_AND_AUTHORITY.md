# Safety, privacy, and authority

PETi enforces authenticated owner and dog scope server-side. Agents receive
least-context bundles and may emit bounded candidate claims, but cannot mutate
care, billing, identity, media, or safety state directly. Deterministic
validation and safety policy run outside the model boundary.

Customer-visible DTOs omit prompts, hidden reasoning, secrets, signed URLs, and
raw internal state. A reminder requires an immutable proposed payload, explicit
approval, and a canonical action receipt. Insufficient evidence, cancellation,
provider failure, and safety routing fail closed or remain visibly partial.
