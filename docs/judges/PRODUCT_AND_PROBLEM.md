# Product and problem

PETi helps a pet owner organize observations, evidence, and follow-up care for
their own dog. The hackathon path is deliberately bounded: compare a fresh
feces image with compatible same-dog history, explain uncertainty, and propose
a reminder that the owner explicitly approves.

PETi is not an autonomous veterinarian and does not replace professional care.
The agent layer reasons over permitted evidence; authenticated PETi services
remain authoritative for identity, dog scope, safety, persistence, and actions.
