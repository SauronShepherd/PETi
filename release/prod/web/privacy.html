PETi Privacy Policy

Status: release-ready source artifact; publication and legal approval are still required.

PETi stores account, pet-profile, care, measurement, media, and veterinary-record information only to provide the features requested by the account holder. Access is authenticated and scoped to the account and authorized collaborators.

AI and media

Optional analysis is performed in controlled cloud services. AI output is treated as an uncertain observation, not a diagnosis or prescription. Media is private by default and retention depends on the feature and the user's choices.

Deletion and export

Account holders can request an export and account deletion from the application. Deletion freezes new work, removes canonical and derived data, removes private media, and verifies that no residual customer payload remains before completion.

Contact

Use the in-app support code to submit a privacy request. A production deployment must replace this placeholder with the approved controller identity, jurisdiction, contact address, effective date, and legal text.
