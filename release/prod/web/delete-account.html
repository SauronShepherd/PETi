Delete your PETi account

Status: release-ready source artifact; production endpoint and identity verification are still required.

To delete your account, sign in to PETi and choose Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Confirm the request when prompted. The request is idempotent and may remain pending while queued work is cancelled and residual data is verified.

Deletion removes your account's pets, media, records, measurements, care history, reports, analysis results, and associated derived data. Existing legal or security retention must be described and approved in the final published policy.

If you cannot sign in, contact support using the production support address and include your PETi support code. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens, or private media by email.
