# Health Apps Declaration Worksheet

Status: `SOURCE_DECLARATION_PENDING_LEGAL_AND_PLAY_REVIEW`

PETi is a pet-care assistance product. It presents owner-entered records,
source-grounded visible observations, uncertainty, safety guidance and prompts
to seek veterinary care. It does not diagnose, prescribe medication or doses,
infer hidden disease, or replace a veterinarian.

Safety controls include deterministic urgency evaluation, abstention when
evidence is insufficient, specialist-specific prohibited-claim filters,
provenance for source media, and explicit limitations in user-visible results.
Provider output is not treated as a clinical authority. Real-provider
calibration and legal/store review remain external release gates.
